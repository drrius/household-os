# Household OS glossary

This glossary defines the version-one product language.

## Household

The private shared space containing the two members, routines, meals, grocery items, and financial ledger.

## Member

One of the two separately identified people in the household.

Both members have equal authority over version-one household data.

## Passkey

A WebAuthn credential registered with Supabase Auth and bound to the application's stable production hostname. It is the normal version-one sign-in method.

## Enrollment link

A short-lived authentication link generated by a trusted administrator command and privately transferred to one confirmed member so that member can register a passkey. It is not sent by email.

## Pet

A lightweight named profile with a photo and linked care routines. A pet is not a member and has no login, financial participation, or medical record in version one.

## Routine

A household responsibility that may be one-off, calendar-based, or completion-based. A routine can be assigned, alternating, or shared and produces occurrences that can be completed.

## Occurrence

One expected instance of a routine, such as cleaning the bathroom this week or brushing the dog's teeth today. It remains open until completed, skipped, or rescheduled.

## Skip

The explicit closure of an occurrence without completion. It preserves the routine's normal cadence and remains visible in history.

## Reschedule

The movement of one occurrence to another due date without changing the routine definition unless the member explicitly chooses to do so.

## Assignment

The responsibility model for a routine: one named member, automatic alternation, or either member.

## Completion

The recorded act of satisfying a routine occurrence, including who completed it and when, with optional notes and photos.

## Meal

A planned or suggested meal. It may occupy an optional breakfast, lunch, or dinner slot; remain in the ideas area; reference an external recipe; and create grocery items or preparation tasks.

## Meal library

Reusable meal definitions containing a name, recipe link, notes, and default grocery items.

## Leftover meal

A planned meal referencing an earlier meal without adding that meal's groceries again.

## Preparation task

A one-off routine occurrence linked to a meal, such as defrosting, chopping, or batch cooking.

## Grocery item

An item requested on the shared grocery list, with optional quantity, unit, category, note, and originating meal. Purchased items leave the active list and remain in recent history for 30 days.

## Shopping trip

A user-confirmed group of purchased grocery items. Finishing a shopping trip may create a draft grocery expense from an entered receipt total and payer.

## Shopping session

The explicit period during which checked grocery items are grouped into one shopping trip. A session ends when a member chooses Finish shopping.

## Expense

A financial event in which one member paid an amount that is allocated between household members.

## Split

The allocation of an expense between members. Version one defaults to 50/50 and permits exact CHF allocations. When an equal split has an odd centime, the payer's own allocation receives the remainder.

## Balance

The CHF amount one member owes the other, derived from expenses, corrections, refunds, and settlements rather than stored as an independently editable number.

## Settlement

A recorded CHF transfer made outside the application that reduces what one member owes the other.

Settlements may be full or partial and are corrected through visible reversing events.

## Refund

A negative financial event linked to an original expense, recording money returned after the original purchase.

## Correction

A visible replacement or adjustment to a financial event. A correction changes the derived balance without erasing the prior recorded state.

## Recurring expense draft

An expected expense created from a schedule. It does not affect the balance until a member confirms its payer, amount, date, and split.

## Opening balance

A visible initial ledger event recording the amount one member owes the other when the household begins using the application.

## Today

The primary daily view, divided into Overdue, Today's routines, Meal and prep, Shopping, and Money requiring attention.

## Activity record

A recoverable history retained for 90 days. It covers routine definition and schedule changes, completions and skips, meal-plan changes, shopping-session completion, and every financial mutation.

## Archive

The reversible removal of a definition, such as a routine, from active use while preserving its historical occurrences and completions.

## Household timezone

The `Europe/Zurich` timezone used to determine household dates regardless of a member's current device timezone.

## Household export

A portable package containing structured JSON, financial CSV, and stored attachments. Household export is excluded from version one.

## Claimed grocery item

An active grocery item currently included in one member's shopping session. It remains visible to the other member but is marked as being handled.

## Receipt total

The full CHF amount shown on an attached shopping receipt. It may differ from the amount allocated as a shared household expense.

## Version-one environment

The single hosted production environment used by the two household members. Local developer instances are not persistent hosted environments.

## Household digest

A per-member summary delivered at that member's configured local time. It includes overdue and due-today routines, today's meals and preparation tasks, whether groceries are active, and pending financial drafts; it excludes the current owed balance.
