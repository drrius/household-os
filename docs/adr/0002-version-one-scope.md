# ADR 0002: Version one covers six connected household domains

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-09

## Context

Household coordination currently spans recurring work, pet care, food planning, shopping, and a separate shared-expense application. The product should provide one coherent place for this work without turning into a general personal productivity suite.

## Decision

Version one will include:

- chores;
- cleaning;
- pet care;
- a shared grocery list;
- a weekly meal board connected to grocery items and preparation tasks; and
- shared expenses that answer who owes whom.

Recipe links are supported. Full recipe management, pantry accounting, nutrition tracking, bank connections, personal budgeting, and household-spending analytics are excluded.

## Consequences

The first release can replace the current combination of informal coordination and Splitwise while retaining a comprehensible boundary. The six domains must share a navigation and interaction model rather than appearing as unrelated mini-apps.
